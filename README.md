#  Supply Chain Analytics Dashboard

![Supply Chain Dashboard](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Khushi-Tipare/Supply-Chain-Management-Analysis/main/Supply%20Chain%20Dashboard.png)

##  Project Overview

The **Supply Chain Analytics Dashboard** is an interactive Power BI project designed to monitor and analyze key supply chain metrics, including sales, inventory, shipping costs, supplier performance, regional sales, and product performance. The dashboard enables data-driven decision-making by providing valuable business insights through dynamic visualizations and KPIs.

---

#  Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales performance across different regions
- Monitor inventory levels and operational costs
- Evaluate supplier performance
- Track shipping costs by transportation mode
- Identify top-performing products and cities
- Analyze monthly sales trends
- Support business decisions with interactive dashboards

#  Dashboard Features

###  Monthly Sales Trend
- Visualizes month-wise sales performance
- Identifies seasonal sales fluctuations
- Helps in demand forecasting

###  Supplier Performance
- Ranks suppliers based on sales contribution
- Identifies top-performing suppliers
- Supports supplier evaluation and decision-making

###  Orders by Shipping Mode
- Distribution of orders across:
  - Road
  - Air
  - Rail
  - Same-day
  - Sea

###  Shipping Cost by Mode
- Compares shipping costs across different transportation modes
- Helps optimize logistics expenses

###  Sales by Region
- Regional sales distribution across:
  - Asia
  - Europe
  - North America
  - South America
  - Middle East

###  Top 10 Cities by Sales
- Identifies highest revenue-generating cities
- Helps understand geographical market performance

###  Top 10 Products by Sales
- Highlights best-selling products
- Supports inventory and product planning

###  Interactive Filters

Users can filter the dashboard by:
- Year
- Ship Mode
- Region
- Product Name
- Category

#  Key Insights

- Generated **100.93M** in total sales revenue from approximately **6K orders**.
- Total operational cost reached **65.33M**, with **9M inventory** under management.
- Road transport accounted for the highest share of orders.
- Air transport incurred the highest shipping cost among transportation modes.
- Apex Global and Axis Partners emerged as the top-performing suppliers.
- São Paulo generated the highest city-wise sales.
- Gaming Laptop Deluxe was the top-selling product.
- Asia contributed the largest share of regional sales.
- Monthly sales showed fluctuations, indicating seasonal demand patterns.
